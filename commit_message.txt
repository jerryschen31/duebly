Root causes (test bugs, not app bugs):

UAT 2/3 — End time never committed. Test relied on clicking outside the picker to blur the time inputs and commit. Real users typically pick from the dropdown listbox or press Enter, sidestepping this. The text-content regex also expected "2:30 pm" while the row renders compact "2:30p".
UAT 6/7 — Swipe race. Two consecutive swipes plus a tab switch raced framer-motion's 170ms commit timeout and the snap-back animation, so the third swipe's drag-end never cleared the threshold.
Fixes in uat.spec.js:

setComposerTimeRange now presses Enter after each time fill (and Escape to close), forcing commits.
